**32. Case Study 1: School Book Fair (Linear Equations)**
A school organized a book fair where entry tickets were priced at ₹$10$ for students and ₹$50$ for adults. At the end of the day, the total amount collected from ticket sales was ₹$2400$. The number of student tickets sold was $3$ times the number of adult tickets sold.

**33. Case Study 2: Park Redesign (Understanding Quadrilaterals)**

*   **i)** What is the sum of the measures of adjacent angles in a parallelogram? *(1 Mark)*
*   **ii)** Find the measure of the smaller angle, $\angle P$. *(1 Mark)*
*   **iii)** Find the measures of all the remaining angles ($\angle Q$, $\angle R$, $\angle S$) and state the property of the parallelogram used to find $\angle R$ and $\angle S$. *(2 Marks)*

**34. Case Study 3: Festive Electronics Sale (Comparing Quantities)**

*   **i)** Calculate the discount amount given on the laptop. *(1 Mark)*
*   **ii)** What is the selling price of the laptop after the discount but before GST is added? *(1 Mark)*

### Section B: 2 Markers

**22.** $5t - 3 = 3t - 5$
$5t - 3t = -5 + 3$

**23.** Sum of exterior angles = $360^\circ$.

**24.** Prime factorization of $729$:
$729 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 = 3^6$

**25.** Let total students be $x$.

**27.** Let breadth = $b$ meters.
Length = $l = 2b + 2$.
Perimeter = $2(l + b) = 154$
$2(2b + 2 + b) = 154$
$3b + 2 = 77$
$3b = 75 \Rightarrow b = 25$ m.

**28.** In parallelogram $ABCD$:
Adjacent angles are supplementary: $\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ \Rightarrow 70^\circ + \angle B = 180^\circ \Rightarrow \angle B = 110^\circ$.
Opposite angles are equal: $\angle C = \angle A = 70^\circ$ and $\angle D = \angle B = 110^\circ$.

**29.** Prime factorization of $252 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times 7$.
The prime factor $7$ does not have a pair. Therefore, multiply by $7$.
New perfect square = $252 \times 7 = 1764$.
Square root of $1764 = 2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$.

**30.** Prime factorization of $13824 = \underline{2 \times 2 \times 2} \times \underline{2 \times 2 \times 2} \times \underline{2 \times 2 \times 2} \times \underline{3 \times 3 \times 3}$

**31.** Total CP = ₹$1200$ + ₹$1200$ = ₹$2400$.
Fan 1 (Loss $5\%$): SP = $1200 - (5\%$ of $1200) = 1200 - 60 = \text{₹}1140$.
Fan 2 (Profit $10\%$): SP = $1200 + (10\%$ of $1200) = 1200 + 120 = \text{₹}1320$.
Total SP = $1140 + 1320 = \text{₹}2460$.
Total Profit = $2460 - 2400 = \text{₹}60$.
Overall Profit $\% = (\frac{60}{2400}) \times 100 = 2.5\%$.

### Section D: 4 Markers (Case Studies)
**32.** 
*   **i)** Number of adult tickets = $x$. Number of student tickets = $3x$. Total collection = Value of adult tickets + Value of student tickets. Equation: $50x + 10(3x) = 2400$.
*   **ii)** $50x + 30x = 2400 \Rightarrow 80x = 2400 \Rightarrow x = 30$.
*   **iii)** Student tickets sold = $3x = 3 \times 30 = 90$. Total tickets sold = $x + 3x = 30 + 90 = 120$ tickets.

**33.**
*   **i)** The sum of the measures of adjacent angles in a parallelogram is $180^\circ$.
*   **ii)** Let angles be $4x$ and $5x$. $4x + 5x = 180^\circ \Rightarrow 9x = 180^\circ \Rightarrow x = 20^\circ$. Smaller angle $\angle P = 4(20^\circ) = 80^\circ$.
*   **iii)** $\angle Q = 5(20^\circ) = 100^\circ$. Property used: Opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal. Therefore, $\angle R = \angle P = 80^\circ$ and $\angle S = \angle Q = 100^\circ$.

**34.**
*   **i)** Discount $= 15\%$ of $40,000 = \frac{15}{100} \times 40,000 = \text{₹}6,000$.
*   **ii)** Selling Price (before GST) = $40,000 - 6,000 = \text{₹}34,000$.
*   **iii)** GST $= 18\%$ of $34,000 = \frac{18}{100} \times 34,000 = \text{₹}6,120$. Total Billing Amount = $34,000 + 6,120 = \text{₹}40,120$.

### Section E: 5 Markers
**35.** Let the total number of deer be $x$.
Grazing in the field = $\frac{x}{2}$.
Remaining = $x - \frac{x}{2} = \frac{x}{2}$.
Playing nearby = $\frac{3}{4}$ of $\frac{x}{2} = \frac{3x}{8}$.
Drinking water = $9$.
Equation: $\frac{x}{2} + \frac{3x}{8} + 9 = x$
Multiply by $8$: $4x + 3x + 72 = 8x$
$7x + 72 = 8x \Rightarrow x = 72$.
Total number of deer = $72$.

**36.** Principal ($P$) = ₹$12,600$, Rate ($R$) = $10\%$ p.a., Time ($n$) = $2$ years.
Amount ($A$) = $P(1 + \frac{R}{100})^n$
$A = 12600 (1 + \frac{10}{100})^2$
$A = 12600 (\frac{11}{10})^2 = 12600 \times \frac{121}{100} = 126 \times 121 = \text{₹}15,246$.
Compound Interest ($CI$) = $A - P = 15246 - 12600 = \text{₹}2,646$.

**37.** Total students = $72$. Central Angle Formula = $\frac{\text{Value}}{\text{Total}} \times 360^\circ$
*   Cricket: $\frac{36}{72} \times 360^\circ = 180^\circ$
*   Football: $\frac{18}{72} \times 360^\circ = 90^\circ$
*   Tennis: $\frac{12}{72} \times 360^\circ = 60^\circ$
*   Badminton: $\frac{6}{72} \times 360^\circ = 30^\circ$
*(Draw a circle and divide it into sectors of 180°, 90°, 60°, and 30° matching the sports).*

**38.** Prime factorization of $53240$:
$53240 = 2 \times 26620$
$= 2 \times 2 \times 13310$
$= 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 6655$
$= 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 5 \times 1331$
$= 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 5 \times 11 \times 11 \times 11$
Grouped as: $\underline{2 \times 2 \times 2} \times 5 \times \underline{11 \times 11 \times 11}$
The prime factor $5$ does not appear in a group of three. So, $53240$ is not a perfect cube.
The smallest whole number by which it must be divided is $5$.
